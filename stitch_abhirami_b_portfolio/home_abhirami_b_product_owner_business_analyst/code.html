Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding roles, availability, workflows, and domain engagements.

I am actively open to Product Owner (PO), Technical Business Analyst (BA), and Agile Delivery roles—especially those working on enterprise AI initiatives, internal toolings, workflow automation, or complex SaaS platforms.
I have steered two flagship enterprise GenAI products from initial concept to live customer production: ChatMCL (a semantic search engine with vector embeddings over complex technical catalogs) and Ask Holley (a conversational pre-purchase advisory agent). My focus is defining RAG parameters, intent handling logic, context limits, evaluation metrics, and ensuring zero-hallucination guardrails for business-critical operations.
I have operated across US Eastern/Central and Indian Standard Time (IST) rhythms throughout my career. I leverage asynchronous documentation standards (detailed Confluence PRDs, recorded Loom walk-throughs, crystal-clear Jira Gherkin tickets) combined with focused high-overlap sync meetings for refinement and unblocking.

Developers receive exact edge cases, validation rules, and error states upfront—which was instrumental in achieving zero-rework sprints for Mini-Circuits.
