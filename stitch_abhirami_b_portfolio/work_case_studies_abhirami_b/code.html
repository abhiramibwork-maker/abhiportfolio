01 / Enterprise GenAI Search & Recommendation Client: Mini-Circuits (US)

ChatMCL: Enterprise RF Component Intelligence

Product Owner · Scrum Master · BA June 2025 – January 2026 Verified Production Delivery
report_problem The Operational Challenge

Engineers and procurement buyers faced an unwieldy technical catalog spanning thousands of RF, IF, and microwave components.

Every specialized impedance, attenuation, and frequency matching query demanded complex multi-parameter manual lookups, averaging over 5 minutes per query. This created friction, lowered conversion rates on complex RF assemblies, and generated high query deflection back to sales engineering desks.

architecture Product Ownership & Agile Architecture
01 / BACKLOG & LIFECYCLE

End-to-End Ownership

Owned the complete product backlog from inception to go-live, defining sprint goals and translating high-level stakeholder vision into technical deliverables.

02 / SPECIFICATIONS

Gherkin BDD Requirements

Structured detailed user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria covering LLM intent parsing, fallback mechanics for out-of-distribution prompts, and relevance reranking.

03 / SCRUM FACILITATION

Cross-Functional Governance

Facilitated agile ceremonies across distributed engineering squads. Resolved architectural ambiguities between Solutions Architects and ML engineers.

04 / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Rigorous UAT Execution

Constructed validation matrices, coordinated stakeholder UAT testing cycles, and defined release gates for precision, hallucination suppression, and system readiness.

02 / GenAI E-Commerce Support & Sales Conversion Client: Holley Automotive (US)

Ask Holley: Intelligent Sales & Fitment Assistant

Product Owner · Scrum Master July 2024 – June 2025 Enterprise Production Deployed
report_problem The Operational Challenge

Thousands of daily automotive compatibility and fitment questions overwhelmed customer support desks.

Inquiries regarding carburetor models, exhaust manifold clearances, and ECU compatibility experienced significant response delays. High ticket queues led directly to cart abandonment, lower CSAT ratings, and missed revenue on high-margin performance components.

view_kanban Stakeholder Alignment & Product Governance
SPRINT RHYTHM Agile Cadence

Led 2-week sprint planning, continuous backlog refinement, and daily standups across cross-functional engineering and data teams.

DISCOVERY Taxonomy Workshops

Facilitated interactive discovery sessions with US client stakeholders to parse intricate aftermarket vehicle compatibility matrices.

STANDARDIZATION Scaled Documentation

Penned the company's first standardized product spec template, subsequently institutionalized across all internal engineering squads.

03 / Analytics & Workflow Self-Serve Automation B2B SaaS Platform

Self-Serve Billing Reporting & Workflow Automation

Product Owner / Business Analyst September 2023 – July 2024 Operational Milestone
report_problem The Operational Challenge

Manual, repetitive report generation tied up dozens of analyst hours every billing cycle.

Enterprise finance and reconciliation teams relied on static spreadsheet dumps requested through an internal ticketing queue. This created a 72-hour bottleneck at end-of-month, introduced human reconciliation discrepancies, and prevented executive stakeholders from viewing real-time invoice telemetry.

insights Business Analysis & Process Re-Engineering
PROCESS MAPPING As-Is vs To-Be

Detailed all friction points, hand-offs, and SQL dependencies in existing manual reconciliation cycles.

FEATURE DESIGN Automated Triggers

Architected custom parameterized scheduling, granular CSV/PDF export jobs, and role-based webhook dispatches.

ADAPTABILITY Agile Scope Steering

Successfully handled two major mid-sprint compliance and ledger scope shifts without delaying release milestones.
